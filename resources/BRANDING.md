# OrganelleScope branding

The application logo was supplied by the project owner as `Gemini_Generated_Image_dqzhhvdqzhhvdqzh.jpg`. `brand-logo.jpg` preserves the supplied artwork. `icon.png` is a square crop of its microscope and cell symbol, converted for app and installer icons. Browser copies are in `frontend/public/branding/`.

Interface palette: navy #001524, teal #15616D, cream #FFECD1, orange #FF7D00 and brown #78290F. Scientific channel colours remain independent of interface branding.
